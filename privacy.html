Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9/8/2026

Messo ("Messo," "we," "us," or "our") provides group messaging delivered through SMS and MMS text messaging (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Phone number — to enable message delivery to and from your groups.
  • Display name — if you set one with #name, so other group members can identify you.
  • Group and membership data — which groups you belong to.
  • Message content — text and media you send, relayed to your group and temporarily stored to enable delivery, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.
  • Message metadata — timestamps, delivery status, and carrier information provided by our messaging provider.

2. How we use information

  • To operate the Service, including routing messages to the right group and members.
  • To prevent abuse and maintain reliability.
  • To comply with carrier and legal requirements, including STOP/START processing.
  • To communicate with you about your account or the Service.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your phone number or other personal information, and we do not share your mobile number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We share information only:

  • With our messaging infrastructure provider, solely to deliver messages.
  • With other members of a group you belong to, to display your messages and display name.
  • When required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Messo or our users.

4. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your group's activity, plus occasional account and service messages.

5. Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

6. Your choices

  • Reply STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, or QUIT at any time to opt out of all Messo messages.
  • Reply START or UNSTOP to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for support.
  • Text #leave <group name> to leave an individual group.

7. Data retention

We retain your phone number and group data for as long as your memberships remain active, and for a limited period afterward for legal, security, or operational purposes.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new effective date.

10. Contact us

Questions? Contact us at privacy@messo.world.
