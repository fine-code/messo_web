Keep friends, family, teams, and communities in one conversation — through ordinary text messages. No app download required.

Messo brings your group together through the phones people already use. Create one conversation, invite people by phone number, and keep everyone included through regular SMS and MMS. A flip phone, an old Android, a new iPhone — it doesn't matter. Use the phone you already have.

Start a group

Bring people in

Share the invite code. They text #join and it, and they're in.

Just text

Every message goes to the whole group, through the texting app you already use.
